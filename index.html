    Your data, plainly

    Privacy Policy

    What FortifyMe collects, why, who sees it, and how to get it changed or deleted.

    Last updated 22 November 2026 Draft

    The short version What we collect How we use it Cookies and storage Emails Who we share it with Children How long we keep it Your rights Security and transfers Changes and contact

    The short version

    • You can browse the shop and build combos without an account.
    • If you sign up, we keep your email address and your locker: favorites, wishlist, saved combos and alert settings.
    • We only email you about your wishlist if you turn alerts on, and you can turn them off any time.
    • We don’t sell your data. Analytics and personalized ads only run if you say yes in Cookie settings.
    • FortifyMe is a fan site. It is not run by, or connected to, Epic Games. We never ask for your Epic account.

    What we collect

    When you create an account

    Your email address and password. Your password is stored only as a scrambled hash by our sign-in provider; nobody at FortifyMe can read it.

    When you use your locker

    The items you favorite or wishlist, the combos you save and their names, whether wishlist alerts are on, and your time zone (so alert emails show shop times in your local time).

    When you contact us

    The topic, your email address, your message, your name if you give one, and the page you sent it from.

    Automatically

    Like most websites, our servers log your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to keep the site running and secure. If you allow analytics, we also count visits and which pages are used. If you allow personalized ads, our ad partner may use cookies or similar identifiers.

    What we don’t collect

    Your Epic Games account, payment details, real name, address, phone number or precise location.

    How we use it

    Cookies and storage

    Essential (always on): your browser stores your locker and your cookie choice, and keeps you signed in. The site can’t work without these.

    Analytics (off unless you allow it): counts visits and page use.

    Personalized ads (off unless you allow it): lets our ad partner choose ads based on your activity. Without it you may still see ads, but they aren’t based on your activity.

    If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as “no” to personalized ads.

    Emails

    We only send wishlist alerts if you turn them on. Each alert tells you how to stop them: remove the item from your wishlist or turn off alerts in My account. We also send emails you need for your account, such as password resets.

    Who we share it with

    We don’t sell your personal information. We use a few service providers that handle data only to run FortifyMe for us:

    • Hosting: serves the website.
    • Database and sign-in: stores your account and locker.
    • Email delivery: sends alert and account emails.
    • Advertising partner (Google AdSense): shows ads, personalized only if you allow it. Ad-free subscribers aren’t sent to it at all.
    • Payments (Stripe): handles ad-free subscription payments. We never see or store your full card number; we keep only your subscription status and Stripe’s customer reference.

    Item images load from fortnite-api.com and the site font loads from Google Fonts, so those services receive your IP address when pages load. We may also share information if the law requires it.

    Children

    Anyone can browse FortifyMe without an account. Children under 13 shouldn’t create an account or send us their email address without a parent or guardian. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us their details, contact us at support@fortifyme.gg and we’ll delete them.

    How long we keep it

    • Account and locker: until you delete your account.
    • Contact messages: up to 12 months.
    • Subscription and payment records: as long as tax and accounting law requires (often up to 7 years), even after you delete your account.
    • Record of alerts sent: up to 12 months, so we don’t email you twice for the same shop appearance.
    • Server logs: up to 30 days.

    Your rights

    You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, and you can withdraw consent at any time (turn off alerts, or change Cookie settings). Depending on where you live, for example in the UK, EU or California, you may have more rights, including objecting to how we use your data and complaining to your local data protection authority.

    To make a request, use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”, or email support@fortifyme.gg. We’ll reply within 30 days.

    Security and transfers

    The site uses HTTPS, passwords are stored only as hashes, and access to your data is limited to what’s needed to run the site. Our service providers may store data in the United States or other countries; where the law requires it, they protect it with standard contractual safeguards.

    Changes and contact

    If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data in a big way, we’ll tell you by email or on the site first.

    Questions? Use the contact form or email support@fortifyme.gg.
